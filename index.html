Why these tools

Most preflight questions come down to a handful of numbers: how much performance you will have at this density altitude, how much crosswind is on this runway, and whether the loaded airplane’s center of gravity is in the envelope. These calculators do that math in one place, with the formulas shown, so you can check an E6B, a flight-planning app, or a paper worksheet.

They are planning aids, not certified avionics. Always defer to the POH or AFM, official weather, and the weight and balance report for the airplane you actually fly.

A flight computer — and an engineering desk — in the browser

Most preflight questions come down to a handful of numbers: how much performance you will have at this density altitude, how much crosswind is on this runway, whether the loaded airplane’s center of gravity is in the envelope, and how much fuel, time, and wind correction the trip will take. The engineering side does the same kind of work for atmosphere properties, lift and drag, compressible flow, rockets, and orbits. The formulas and assumptions are on each page, so you can check an E6B, a worksheet, or a textbook example.

They are planning aids, not certified avionics. Always defer to the POH or AFM, official weather, and the weight and balance report for the airplane you actually fly.

Frequently asked questions

  • Are these aviation calculators free?

    Yes. Every calculator here is free to use in your browser. There is no account, no install, and no upload. The pages may show advertisements to keep the tools free.

  • What is an E6B or flight computer?

    An E6B is the classic circular flight computer pilots use for wind correction and groundspeed, true airspeed, time-speed-distance, fuel, and unit conversions. These pages do that same work as an online E6B, with the formulas shown.

  • Do you have aerospace engineering calculators as well as pilot tools?

    Yes. The site is organized in two sides. Pilot tools cover flight planning, performance, weather, and regulations. Aerospace engineering tools cover the standard atmosphere, aerodynamics, propulsion, and orbital mechanics. Some calculators, such as the standard atmosphere and true airspeed, appear in both.

  • Do I need an account or an app?

    No. The calculators are static web pages. All of the math runs locally in your browser, so nothing you type is sent to a server.

  • Can I use these instead of my POH or official weather?

    No. These are planning aids, not certified avionics. Always defer to the Pilot’s Operating Handbook or Aircraft Flight Manual, official weather, and the weight and balance report for the airplane you actually fly.

  • Do the calculators work on a phone?

    Yes. Every tool is built to work on a phone, tablet, or desktop. Use the search box above to jump to a calculator by name or by a term such as density altitude, standard atmosphere, or METAR decoder.

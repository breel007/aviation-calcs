Brayton Cycle Calculator

The ideal air-standard Brayton cycle — the textbook model of a simple turbojet or gas turbine — has a thermal efficiency that depends only on the compressor pressure ratio: η = 1 − 1 / r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}. Enter a pressure ratio (and optionally inlet and turbine-inlet temperatures) and this page returns that efficiency, the isentropic station temperatures, and the net work and heat input per kilogram. It is the ideal cycle, not a real engine.

γ defaults to 1.4 (cold air). A pressure ratio of 10 then has η ≈ 48.2%. Temperatures are optional; if you enter T1, T2 follows from the isentropic relation, and T3 (turbine inlet) produces T4, heat in, and net work.

Efficiency is fixed by the pressure ratio

For constant specific heats and isentropic compression and expansion, the Brayton thermal efficiency is η = 1 − 1 / r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}. At r_p = 10 and γ = 1.4 that is about 48.2%. If you enter a compressor-inlet temperature T1, the isentropic compressor exit is T2 = T1 r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}; an optional turbine-inlet T3 then gives T4 = T3 / r_p^{(γ−1)/γ}, a heat input c_p(T3−T2), and a net work c_p((T3−T4) − (T2−T1)). The efficiency implied by those temperatures matches the pressure-ratio formula on the ideal cycle. This page uses c_p = 1,004.5 J/(kg·K) for the optional work numbers only.

A real engine has burner pressure loss, cooling bleed, and component efficiencies that all cut η. For a control-volume thrust from mass flow and velocity, see jet engine thrust; for a rocket, see specific impulse.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t T3 appear in the simple efficiency formula?

On the ideal cycle with constant cp, η depends only on the pressure ratio (or equivalently on T1/T2). T3 changes the work and the heat input in the same proportion.

What if T3 is lower than T2?

That is not a heat-engine cycle — you would be “adding” negative heat. The page rejects it.

Is this a turbofan or a ramjet?

It is the ideal Brayton (gas-turbine) cycle. A ramjet is a related Brayton process without the turbomachinery, and a turbofan adds a bypass stream this page does not model.

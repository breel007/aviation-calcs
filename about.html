About Aviation Calcs

Aviation Calcs is a set of 78 free, mobile-friendly aviation calculators that run entirely in your browser. They cover the numbers most of us reach for before a flight and around the hangar: an online E6B for wind, fuel, true airspeed, and time-speed-distance; performance tools for density altitude, crosswind, weight and balance, glide distance, and climb gradient; weather helpers including METAR and TAF decoders, cloud base, and sunrise, sunset, and FAA night; and cost estimators for aircraft ownership, loans, flight training, and rentals, plus an FAA pilot currency checker. Browse all 78 tools on the home page.

They were built by a commercial pilot, CFII, and flight test engineer who got tired of burying a phone calculator under an E6B and a POH. The goal is straightforward math, visible formulas, and enough explanation that you can check the answer — not a black box, and not a substitute for the book.

Nothing you type is uploaded. Weight-and-balance loadouts, if you save them, stay in this browser. The calculators are planning and training aids only. Always defer to the Pilot’s Operating Handbook or Aircraft Flight Manual, official weather, and the official weight and balance report for the airplane you fly.
